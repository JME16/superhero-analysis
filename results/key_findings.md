## Villain Threat Analysis

### Question 1: Which villains are associated with the greatest actual damage?

**Finding:** Darkseid ranks first with $4.7 billion in actual damage, followed by Brainiac at $2.4 billion.

**Interpretation:** These villains represent the greatest cumulative damage exposure in the recorded missions. Further analysis is needed to distinguish incident severity from mission frequency.

### Question 2: Which event types produce the largest financial exposure?

**Finding:** Planetary / Catastrophic Threats generated $7.1 billion
in recorded villain damage across two incidents, averaging $3.55
billion per incident. City-Scale Assaults ranked second with
$2.215 billion across three incidents.

**Interpretation:** Planetary threats lead because of incident
severity rather than frequency. Urban Combat occurred five times
but generated substantially less damage. Geographic scope may
help explain the difference, but requires further evidence.

**Limitation:** Operating and repair costs are reported separately
because combining them with damage may double-count exposure.
